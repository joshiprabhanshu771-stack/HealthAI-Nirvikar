1-Minute Donor Eligibility Quiz

Answer 4 simple clinical questions to verify if you can safely donate blood today.

1. Are you between 18 and 65 years of age?

2. Do you weigh at least 45 to 50 kg (100 lbs)?

3. Have you donated whole blood in the last 90 days (3 months)?

4. Have you had major surgery, fever/infection, or a tattoo in the past 6 months?
