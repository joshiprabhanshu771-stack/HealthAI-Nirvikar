Trimester-by-Trimester Journey

Track physiological changes, fetal growth milestones, and clinical screening recommendations.

First Trimester: The Foundation Phase

Weeks 1 to 12 • Embryo to Fetus
Critical Neural Tube Window

Baby Development

During weeks 1–12, single cells multiply into a fully formed fetus. The heart begins beating by week 5–6, facial features emerge, and the neural tube forms the brain and spinal cord. Tiny fingers, toes, and eyelids are formed by week 10.

Maternal Body Changes

A surge in hCG and progesterone hormones causes nausea (morning sickness), heightened olfactory sensitivity, tender swollen breasts, frequent urination, and profound fatigue as your body creates the placenta.

Nutritional Priority

  • Folic Acid (400–600 mcg): Essential for preventing spinal bifida and anencephaly.
  • Vitamin B6 & Ginger: Helps naturally alleviate morning sickness.
  • Small, Frequent Meals: Keeps blood sugar stable and avoids stomach distress.

Key Clinical Tests

  • Dating & Viability Ultrasound (Weeks 6–8)
  • Nuchal Translucency (NT) Scan & Dual Marker Blood Test (Weeks 11–13)
  • Blood Grouping, Rh Factor, Hemoglobin & Thyroid Panel (TSH)

Second Trimester: The Golden Period

Weeks 13 to 26 • Rapid Growth & First Kicks
Resurgence of Energy

Baby Development

Baby can hear maternal voices and heartbeats, swallow amniotic fluid, practice thumb-sucking, and blink. Distinct fingerprints and lanugo hair develop. Between weeks 18–22, you will feel initial flutter kicks ("quickening").

Maternal Body Changes

Nausea usually subsides and energy returns. The baby bump expands upward, ligaments stretch (mild round ligament pain), skin may develop a pregnancy glow or linea nigra, and appetite increases.

Nutritional Priority

  • Elemental Iron (27 mg/day): Powers red blood cells for increased blood volume.
  • Calcium (1000 mg) & Vitamin D: Ensures mineral density for developing bones.
  • DHA (200–300 mg): Essential for fetal brain and retinal growth.

Key Clinical Tests

  • Comprehensive Level-II Target Anomaly Scan (Weeks 18–20)
  • Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT) for Gestational Diabetes (Weeks 24–28)
  • Routine Blood Pressure & Urine Albumin checks at every visit

Third Trimester: The Final Countdown

Weeks 27 to 40+ • Maturation & Birth Prep
Labor & Delivery Readiness

Baby Development

Lungs produce surfactant to prepare for breathing air, eyes react to light, fat layers accumulate for temperature regulation, and baby practices rhythmic breathing motions while turning head-down into the pelvis.

Maternal Body Changes

Shortness of breath as the uterus presses the diaphragm, mild pelvic pressure, irregular practice contractions (Braxton Hicks), frequent urination, swollen ankles, and nesting urges.

Nutritional Priority

  • Lean Protein (75–100 g/day): Supports rapid final fetal weight gain.
  • Fiber & Fluids: Prevents constipation and hemorrhoids.
  • Magnesium & Potassium: Reduces leg cramps and optimizes muscle rest.

Key Clinical Tests

  • Growth Ultrasound & Fetal Doppler (Weeks 32–36)
  • Group B Streptococcus (GBS) Swab (Weeks 36–37)
  • Weekly or Bi-weekly Antenatal Checkups from Week 36 onwards

Antenatal Care (ANC) & Vaccination Schedule

Standard World Health Organization (WHO) and Ministry of Health clinical timeline for visits and immunizations.

Visit 1 • Weeks 6–8

Confirmation & Viability

Dating ultrasound, blood typing, Rh factor test, complete blood count, and starting daily Folic Acid supplements.

Visit 2 • Weeks 11–14

NT Scan & 1st Td Vaccine

Nuchal Translucency screening, Dual Marker blood test, First dose of Tetanus Toxoid (Td) immunization.

Visit 3 • Weeks 18–22

Level II Anomaly Scan

Detailed anatomical scan of all fetal organs, 2nd Td vaccine dose (4 weeks after 1st), and cervical length check.

Visit 4 • Weeks 28–36+

OGTT, Tdap & Growth Scan

Gestational diabetes oral glucose screen (OGTT), Tdap booster (Wk 28–36), fetal growth doppler, and labor birth plan.

Safe Exercises & Sleep Posture

Staying moderately active supports circulation, stamina for labor, and alleviates back pain.

Brisk Walking (20–30 mins)

Low-impact aerobic exercise that maintains cardiovascular tone without joint stress.

Pelvic Floor (Kegel) Exercises

Strengthens bladder control and pelvic muscles to assist during natural labor delivery.

Left-Side Sleeping (SOS Position)

Maximizes blood flow and nutrient delivery through the inferior vena cava to the placenta.

Government Maternal Schemes & Benefits

Financial assistance and institutional delivery benefits provided by the Government of India.

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

Direct cash incentive of ₹5,000 in installments for pregnant women & lactating mothers to compensate wage loss and ensure prenatal nutrition.

Eligibility: First & second live birth upon MCP card registration.

Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)

Safe motherhood intervention providing financial assistance of ₹1,400 (Rural) / ₹1,000 (Urban) along with free transport via Janani Express (102).

Benefits: Free institutional delivery & post-natal care at public health centers.
