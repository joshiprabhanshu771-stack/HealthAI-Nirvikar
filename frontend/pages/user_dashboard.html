Emergency Protocol

First Aid & Emergency Guide

Immediate, step-by-step guidance for critical medical situations before emergency services arrive.

Burns

Cool the burn with cool running water for at least 10 minutes. Cover loosely with sterile cling film.

Learn More

Cuts & Bleeding

Apply firm direct pressure with a clean cloth. Elevate the injured limb above heart level if possible.

Learn More

Choking

Perform 5 sharp back blows between shoulder blades followed by 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver).

Learn More

CPR (Adult)

Push hard and fast in the center of the chest at 100-120 compressions per minute until help arrives.

Learn More

Fractures

Immobilize the injured area. Do not attempt to push exposed bones back into place.

Learn More

Heat Stroke

Move person to a cool area, apply cold damp cloths, and sip water slowly if conscious.

Learn More

Electric Shock

Do not touch the victim while connected to current. Turn off power source immediately.

Learn More

Snake Bite

Keep person calm and immobilized. Remove tight jewelry and seek urgent medical anti-venom.

Learn More
Public Healthcare

Government Healthcare Schemes

Discover universal health protection policies, financial aid, and subsidized treatment initiatives.

National Cover

Ayushman Bharat (PM-JAY)

Provides secondary and tertiary hospitalization coverage up to ₹5 Lakh per family annually.

Eligibility: Low-income families listed under SECC database.
Benefits: Cashless access to 27,000+ empanelled hospitals.
Required Docs: Aadhaar Card, Ration Card, PM-JAY letter.
Learn More & Apply
Maternal Health

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana

Direct cash incentive scheme of ₹5,000 for pregnant women and lactating mothers.

Eligibility: First live birth, age 19 years and above.
Benefits: Compensation for wage loss & nutritional support.
Required Docs: Mother & Child Protection (MCP) card, ID proof.
Learn More & Apply
Affordable Medicine

Jan Aushadhi Yojana

Provides quality generic medicines at prices 50% to 90% lower than branded market equivalents.

Eligibility: Open to all citizens of India.
Benefits: Access to 1,800+ medicines and 290 surgical items.
Required Docs: Valid doctor prescription.
Learn More & Apply
