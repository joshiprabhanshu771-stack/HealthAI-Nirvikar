Child Health & Pediatric Care

Evidence-informed pediatric guidance from infancy through adolescence: developmental milestones, universal immunizations, fever triage, age-stage nutrition, and emergency first aid.

Pediatric Fever & Triage

Children are physiologically distinct from adults. Enter your child's age and temperature for immediate pediatric assessment.

Triage Assessment

Moderate Fever

Elevated Pediatric Temperature (101.2°F)

The body is fighting an active infection (often viral cold, flu, or ear infection).

Recommended Action: Sponge with lukewarm water (never cold water). Offer plenty of fluids. Consult your pediatrician for weight-accurate paracetamol dosage.

Clinical Rule: Any fever ≥ 100.4°F in an infant under 3 months is an immediate medical emergency.

Developmental Milestone Explorer

Track essential pediatric growth leaps across physical, cognitive, speech, and emotional domains.

Newborn & Early Infant Reflexes

Age Group: 0-3 Months
Standard Milestone

Motor & Physical Skills

Lifts head briefly while on tummy; moves arms and legs equally; opens and shuts hands; strong rooting and grasp reflexes.

Cognitive & Speech

Startles at loud sounds; turns head toward maternal voice; coos and makes pleasant gurgling sounds; follows moving faces with eyes.

Social & Emotional

Develops first spontaneous and responsive social smiles (by 6-8 weeks); calms to gentle touch or rocking; holds eye contact.

Developmental Red Flags (Consult Pediatrician If Present):

Does not react to loud noises; does not follow moving objects with eyes; does not smile by 8 weeks; floppy or excessively stiff muscle tone.

Parenting & Stimulation Tip: Encourage 3–5 minutes of supervised tummy time daily when awake; sing and talk gently during diaper changes.

Age-Stage Pediatric Nutrition Guide

Age-appropriate feeding guidelines: from exclusive breastfeeding to toddler balanced meals.

0 to 6 Months

Exclusive Milk Phase

Exclusive breastfeeding or infant formula provides 100% of required hydration, electrolytes, and antibodies.

  • Strictly NO plain water, cow's milk, or honey before 6 months.
  • Feed on demand (every 2-3 hours for newborns).
  • Daily 400 IU Vitamin D supplementation.
6 to 12 Months

Introduction of Solids

Start complementary feeding while continuing breastmilk/formula as the primary calorie source.

  • Iron-rich single-ingredient purees (moong dal, ragi, mashed banana, apple).
  • 3-Day Wait Rule to monitor for food allergies.
  • Introduce small sips of boiled water with meals.
1 to 3 Years

Toddler Balanced Plate

Transition to family meals with 3 small meals and 2 nutritious snacks daily.

  • Whole pasteurized cow's milk (max 500 ml/day to avoid iron-deficiency anemia).
  • Finger foods, vegetables, curd, eggs, paneer.
  • Avoid choking hazards (whole grapes, nuts, hard candies).
4 to 12 Years

School-Age Growth Fuel

Balanced energy for cognitive focus in school, muscular growth, and athletic activity.

  • Complex carbohydrates (whole grains, oats) for sustained energy.
  • Calcium & Vitamin D for bone density growth spurts.
  • Limit sugary sodas and packaged ultra-processed snacks.

Common Childhood Illnesses & Home Care

Verified clinical guidelines on symptom recognition, safe home care, and emergency red flags.

Pediatric Fever (Pyrexia)

Symptoms: Body temp > 100.4°F, warm forehead, irritability, flushed cheeks.
Home Care: Light cotton clothing, lukewarm sponge, fluid hydration, paracetamol under pediatric advice.
Danger Signs: Fever in infants < 3 months; fever > 104°F; stiff neck; seizure; non-blanching purple spots.

Acute Diarrhea & Vomiting

Symptoms: Watery stools (>3/day), mild stomach cramps, nausea, mild fever.
Home Care: Start Oral Rehydration Salts (ORS) immediately after every loose stool; give Zinc drops for 14 days.
Danger Signs: Sunken eyes/fontanelle, absence of tears, no urination for >6h, blood in stool.

Cold, Cough & Croup

Symptoms: Stuffy/runny nose, sneezing, mild cough, barking seal-like cough in croup.
Home Care: Saline nasal drops before feeds; cool mist humidifier; warm fluids (honey for children >1 yr).
Danger Signs: Rapid breathing (>50/min), ribs sucking in (chest indrawing), stridor whistling, blue lips.

Ear Infection (Otitis Media)

Symptoms: Ear tugging in infants, night crying, fluid draining from ear canal, irritability.
Home Care: Warm cloth compress over outer ear; feed infant at 45° upright angle; doctor consultation.
Danger Signs: Swelling/redness behind the ear (mastoid area), high fever, foul-smelling pus discharge.

Childhood Rashes (HFM, Eczema)

Symptoms: Itchy patches (Eczema), blisters on palms/soles/mouth (Hand-Foot-Mouth), prickly heat.
Home Care: Fragrance-free gentle moisturizers, loose cotton clothing, keep child's nails short.
Danger Signs: Rapidly spreading rash with high fever, purple spots that do not fade when pressed (petechiae).

Emergency Protocol: Pediatric Choking Action

Follow the correct age-specific sequence immediately when an airway is obstructed.

Infant Choking (< 1 Year Old)

  1. 5 Back Blows: Lay infant face down along your forearm, supporting head and jaw with your hand. Deliver 5 firm back blows between shoulder blades with heel of hand.
  2. 5 Chest Thrusts: Turn infant face up onto your other forearm (head lower than chest). Give 5 quick downward chest thrusts using 2 fingers in center of breastbone.
  3. Repeat Cycle: Continue alternating 5 back blows and 5 chest thrusts until object is expelled or baby cries.
Warning: NEVER perform blind finger sweeps in an infant's mouth, as this can push the object deeper.

Child Choking (> 1 Year Old)

  1. 5 Back Blows: Lean the child forward and deliver 5 sharp blows between shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
  2. Heimlich Abdominal Thrusts: Stand behind child, place a fist just above the navel, grasp with your other hand, and give 5 quick upward & inward thrusts.
  3. Emergency Call: If child loses consciousness, call 108 immediately and begin pediatric CPR (30 compressions : 2 rescue breaths).
Emergency: Dial 108 / 112 immediately if obstruction does not clear in 1 minute.

Government Child Healthcare Schemes

National healthcare initiatives and free child development programs by the Government of India.

Universal Immunization

Mission Indradhanush

Ensures 100% free immunization coverage for all infants, children, and pregnant women against 12 vaccine-preventable life-threatening diseases.

Early Intervention

Rashtriya Bal Swasthya (RBSK)

Free comprehensive child health screening for the "4 Ds": Defects at birth, Deficiencies, Childhood Diseases, and Developmental delays/disabilities.

Nutrition Security

POSHAN Abhiyaan

National flagship initiative combating child malnutrition, stunting, under-nutrition, and low birth weight through Anganwadi community centers.
